Phi-Scaled Resonant Toroidal Research Platform

Experimental Research by R. Wierzbicki · USA · 2026

---

Overview

This repository documents the design, fabrication, and experimental evaluation of a phi-scaled toroidal resonator platform utilizing non-periodic winding geometry, Fibonacci-derived dimensional relationships, and multi-coil harmonic coupling.

The project investigates whether recursively scaled toroidal structures and quasi-periodic winding distributions exhibit measurable differences in:

- resonance behavior
- harmonic distribution
- inductive coupling
- impedance characteristics
- field coherence
- transfer efficiency

compared to conventional toroidal winding architectures.

The current focus is experimental and exploratory. No claims of overunity performance or violation of established physical laws are made.

---

Research Objectives

The platform is designed to investigate several specific questions:

1. Do phi-scaled toroidal geometries alter resonant behavior compared to standard toroids?

2. Can quasi-periodic winding offsets reduce parasitic harmonic interference?

3. Do nested harmonic winding relationships influence coupling efficiency or phase stability?

4. Are there measurable differences in:
   
   - Q factor
   - resonance bandwidth
   - impedance response
   - thermal behavior
   - harmonic persistence
   - near-field distribution patterns

5. Can non-uniform winding architectures improve wireless or near-field energy transfer efficiency?

Winding Architecture

Winding| Gauge| Direction| Offset
Primary| 18 AWG| Clockwise| 0°
Counter| 18 AWG| Counter-clockwise| 120°
Harmonic| 20 AWG| Offset winding| 137.5°

The harmonic winding utilizes a golden-angle offset intended to distribute winding density non-uniformly around the toroidal surface.

One hypothesis under investigation is that quasi-periodic winding distributions may alter standing-wave formation, harmonic overlap, or localized field concentration compared to evenly periodic winding arrangements.
